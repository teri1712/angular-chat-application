## 3. Logo Usage

### Concept
The logo should represent **Speed** and **Connection**.
- A stylized "T" that doubles as a lightning bolt or a chat bubble trail.
- Forward-leaning italicized typography to imply motion.

---

## 4. Voice & Tone

### Brand Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Energetic** | High-tempo, exciting, and "live". |
| **Direct** | No fluff, just what you need to know, fast. |
| **Minimalist** | Cutting through the noise to focus on the conversation. |
| **Powerful** | Built for heavy users who need speed and scale. |

### Voice Chart

| Trait | We Are | We Are Not |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Energetic | Fast, responsive | Hyper, annoying |
| Direct | Concise, clear | Blunt, rude |
| Minimalist | Essential, clean | Empty, boring |
| Powerful | Capable, robust | Complex, slow |

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Marketing | Hype-focused, fast | "Chat at the speed of thought." |
| App Interface | Invisible, functional | "Message sent." |
| Error messages | Quick, recovery-focused | "Reconnecting..." |

---

## 5. Imagery Guidelines

### Photography Style
- **Motion Blur:** Use subtle motion blur to imply speed.
- **Vibrant Lighting:** High contrast, neon accents.
- **Real People:** Authentic, candid shots of people connecting.

---

## 6. Design Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Electric Blue background, rounded corners (12px), bold text.
- **Secondary:** Outlined Electric Blue or Glassmorphism effect.

### Border Radius
- **Cards:** 24px (Modern, friendly)
- **Buttons/Inputs:** 12px
- **Avatar:** Circular
